About the Stapleton Public Library portal

This branded experience highlights how Stapleton Public Library can guide patrons through collections, events, programs, and research support online.

What the portal includes

  • A Stapleton Public Library home page with guided chat prompts.
  • Catalog browsing with filters and detailed record cards.
  • An SPL events calendar with recurring program highlights.
  • Program categories for Youth Services, Adult Services, Tech Team, and Special Collections.

Service notes

  • Account pages are simulated and non-personalized for safe demonstration.
  • Pericles is presented and named throughout as the AI support client for Stapleton Public Library.
  • The portal emphasizes public service, research support, and community access.

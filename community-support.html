Stapleton community resources

Find support in your community

Housing, food, recovery, family safety, early learning, and disability support. Ask Pericles to help you choose a starting point.

Service hours are local to Stapleton. Contact provider intake to confirm current capacity, supplies, or enrollment. Pericles can explain your options and help prepare questions.

Harbor House Community Services

Who it serves: Adults experiencing homelessness or at risk of losing housing.

Day shelter, meals, showers, laundry, mail access, housing navigation, and referrals for overnight shelter.

Start here: Visit the Harbor House public day-center reception and ask for a housing navigator. Tell them whether you need somewhere to stay tonight.

Hours: Day-center walk-ins: Monday–Friday, 8 AM–5 PM.

Family Bridge of Stapleton

Who it serves: Families with children who need stable housing.

Family shelter intake, housing case management, help keeping families together, and links to childcare and school support.

Start here: Ask the Family Bridge intake desk for a family housing assessment; explain how many adults and children need shelter.

Hours: Intake: Monday–Friday, 9 AM–4 PM.

Willow Haven Survivor Services

Who it serves: People affected by domestic or dating violence, sexual assault, or coercive control, including women and their children.

Confidential advocacy, safety planning, emergency shelter screening, court accompaniment, and support groups. Survivors of any gender can request help.

Start here: Ask for an advocate at the public Willow Haven advocacy office. Shelter locations are not public. You do not need to describe the abuse in this chat.

Hours: Public advocacy-office walk-ins: Monday–Friday, 9 AM–5 PM.

Open Door Recovery Network

Who it serves: Adults seeking help with alcohol or other drug use, and family members looking for support.

Treatment navigation, screening referrals, connections to licensed outpatient or residential treatment, and insurance or payment-resource guidance.

Start here: Ask the Open Door intake desk for a treatment-navigation conversation. Ask specifically about medically supervised withdrawal care if that is what you need.

Hours: Navigation walk-ins: Monday–Friday, 9 AM–5 PM.

Next Step Peer Collective

Who it serves: People in recovery or considering a change in substance use.

Peer recovery coaching, support groups, practical goal planning, and harm-reduction education. This is peer support, not a medical detox facility.

Start here: Visit the Next Step community room and ask about a peer navigator or the current group schedule.

Hours: Peer drop-in: Monday–Saturday, noon–6 PM.

Stapleton Community Table

Who it serves: Households that need groceries or cannot cover both food and basic expenses.

Choice-based grocery pantry, hygiene supplies when stocked, and help finding food-benefit application support.

Start here: Visit the Community Table pantry welcome desk. No photo ID or proof of income is required; staff ask household size to plan groceries.

Hours: Pantry shopping: Tuesday and Thursday, noon–6 PM; Saturday, 10 AM–1 PM.

Neighbor’s Kitchen

Who it serves: Anyone needing a prepared meal.

Free prepared community meals and information about other food resources.

Start here: Come to the Neighbor’s Kitchen meal hall during serving hours. No registration is required.

Hours: Community meal: daily, noon–1:30 PM.

Bright Beginnings Community Action

Who it serves: Families seeking early childhood education and family support.

Head Start for children ages 3–5 and Early Head Start for pregnant people and children under 3; developmental screenings, family support, and enrollment assistance.

Start here: Ask the Bright Beginnings enrollment office for an eligibility review and current application checklist. Income and other eligibility rules apply; enrollment depends on an available place.

Hours: Enrollment help: Monday–Friday, 8:30 AM–4:30 PM.

Stapleton Autism and Family Resource Center

Who it serves: Autistic children and adults, people seeking evaluation information, and families or caregivers.

Autism information, family navigation, sensory-friendly activities, peer groups, and referrals to licensed assessment and therapy providers.

Start here: Ask the resource-center welcome desk for a family or adult-services navigator. A diagnosis is not required for an initial information conversation.

Hours: Resource navigation: Monday–Friday, 10 AM–4 PM.

Common Ground Disability Alliance

Who it serves: People with developmental disabilities and their families, including autistic adults.

Self-advocacy, independent-living support, benefits navigation, employment support, and connections to disability services.

Start here: Visit the Common Ground resource desk and ask for an intake conversation about your goals and support needs.

Hours: Resource desk: Monday–Friday, 9 AM–4 PM.

Stapleton Community Legal Aid

Who it serves: Residents with limited income who need civil legal assistance.

Screening for eviction, housing conditions, benefits appeals, family safety, and other civil legal issues. Representation depends on eligibility and capacity.

Start here: Ask the legal-aid intake desk about the relevant civil issue and any hearing or filing deadline. Do not post documents or identifying case details here.

Hours: Intake: Tuesday–Thursday, 10 AM–3 PM.

Neighborhood Family Resource Exchange

Who it serves: Families needing basic supplies or help navigating assistance.

Diapers, period supplies, household essentials when stocked, and help finding utility and family-support programs.

Start here: Ask the Family Resource Exchange welcome desk what supplies are currently available and about assistance with utility applications.

Hours: Walk-ins: Monday, Wednesday, and Friday, 10 AM–4 PM.

Stapleton VA Outpatient Clinic

Government health service for veterans seeking enrollment guidance, primary care, and referrals.

Start here: Ask the enrollment and patient-services desk about eligibility, enrollment documents, and appointment options.

Hours: Enrollment desk Monday–Friday, 8 AM–4:30 PM. Clinical appointments are scheduled separately.

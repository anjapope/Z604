Community Calendar

Stapleton Public Library events and programs

Recurring SPL events, seasonal highlights, and public meetings for families, teens, and adults.

Library hours

  • Monday-Friday: 8:00 AM-8:00 PM
  • Saturday: 8:00 AM-5:00 PM
  • Sunday: 12:00 PM-4:00 PM

Weekly recurring events

  • Storytime: Tuesdays and Fridays, 10:00-11:00 AM
  • Tech Team Training Seminars: Mondays, 11:00 AM (open to the public)
  • Adult Book Club: Wednesdays, 6:30 PM
  • Fiction Convention Showcase: Fridays, 6:30 PM
  • Teen Game and Craft Days/Tournaments: Saturdays, 12:00-4:00 PM

Monthly recurring events

  • Lego Lab: 4th Thursday, 5:30-7:30 PM (families, kids, enthusiasts)
  • Board Meeting: 4th Thursday, 8:00 PM (open to the public)

Alternating-month workshops

  • Digital Citizenship Workshop: Tuesdays at 7:30 PM in every other month (all ages)

Seasonal and special programs

  • Summer Reading Program: June 1-July 31 with prizes; families sign up in the BeanStack app.
  • Frontiers and Folktales: Runs through Fall in partnership with Youth Services, Special Collections, and Mercer Park; features oral renditions of regional folktales.
  • Youth Services Monster Prom: Weekend before Halloween, 8:00-10:30 PM.

Instructions

How to use the Stapleton Public Library portal

  1. Start on the home page, where Pericles opens with a Stapleton Public Library welcome message.
  2. Select one of the quick-action prompts to explore a common SPL question.
  3. Or type your own natural-language prompt about collections, events, programs, or reference help.
  4. Review the response and continue the conversation as needed.

Use this portal to explore Stapleton Public Library services and manage your account.

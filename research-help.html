Special Collections Department

In-person research help

Meet with Logan King, Special Collections (SPC) librarian and Head of Research and Genealogical Services, for help with your research at Stapleton Public Library.

Limited research service hours

In-person assistance from Logan King is available only during these weekly hours through the Special Collections Department:

  • Tuesday: 11:30 AM–1:00 PM
  • Sunday: 2:00–4:00 PM

All times are local to Stapleton (America/Indiana/Indianapolis). These are research service hours, separate from the library's general opening hours. Logan is not available for research consultations outside these times.

Research and genealogical services

  • Family history: Develop a genealogical question and identify useful records.
  • Local history: Explore Stapleton's history through archives and special collections.
  • Research planning: Refine a topic, build search terms, and identify sources.
  • Working with evidence: Evaluate sources, track findings, and develop citations.

Bring your research question, notes on what you have already tried, and any relevant names, dates, or places so you can make the most of the available time.

Need help sooner? Need help with your humanities homework? Try visiting the least human member of our team—Pericles! SPL's very own AI Reference librarian!
